## Brand & Style

This design system translates the precision of deep-sea acoustic monitoring into an airy, high-clarity laboratory aesthetic. It bridges acoustic engineering and maritime cartography, projecting scientific rigor, spatial precision, and high-frequency analytical focus. 

The visual style blends **Technical Minimalism** with **Subtle High-Contrast Instrumentation**:
- **Purity & Clarity:** Immaculate light backdrops evoke cleanroom monitors and calibrated hydrographic charts.
- **Oceanic Acoustics:** Deep sub-surface teals anchor critical interaction paths, punctuated by solar gold pings indicating telemetry locks, alerts, and vital transient phenomena.
- **Micro-Precision:** Fine hair-width structural division lines, technical monospaced telemetry tags, and generous breathing room maintain legibility across dense waveforms, depth profiles, and real-time spectrum metrics.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to exceed WCAG AAA accessibility thresholds across all core textual and semantic interaction tokens over white and off-white base planes.

### Primary Accents (Abyssal Teal & Cyan)
- **Primary Teal (`#007b83`):** Primary command buttons, selected states, and dominant waveform readouts. Meets AAA contrast (minimum 7:1) against `#ffffff`.
- **Secondary Ocean Cyan (`#0e7490`):** Secondary controls, active tab indicators, and spectrum depth markers.
- **Interactive Vivid Teal (`#0d9488`):** Focus rings, active hover transitions, and dynamic frequency pings.

### Energy Accent (Solar Amber)
- **Solar Gold (`#b45309`):** Warnings, critical acoustic thresholds, highlighted metadata, and signal transient tags. Delivers AAA contrast (approx. 7.3:1) on white.
- **Solar Radiant (`#d97706`):** Secondary state indicator for non-critical alerts and secondary graphical anomalies.

### Base & Technical Neutrals
- **Background Root (`#ffffff`):** Pure pristine canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#f8fafc`):** Card panels, sidebar navigation, and data inspect wells.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#f1f5f9`):** Grouped item containers, table headers, and sunken telemetry bays.
- **Structural Border (`#e2e8f0`):** Crisp, hairline dividers and perimeter enclosures.
- **Text Primary (`#0f172a`):** Deep abyssal slate providing maximum readability (>14:1 contrast).
- **Text Secondary (`#1e293b`):** Subheaders, metadata keys, and secondary descriptors.
- **Text Muted (`#334155`):** Inactive icons, timestamps, units of measure, and axis labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies entirely on **Space Grotesk** across display, body, and micro-metric labels to emphasize algorithmic precision, technical heritage, and sharp structural geometric cadence.

- **Headlines & Displays:** Tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) offsets Space Grotesk’s natural wide footprint, keeping titles compact and impactful.
- **Body Elements:** Standard weight `400` with generous line-height provides unhindered scanning for technical logs, hydro-acoustic reports, and telemetry telemetry data.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Set with increased tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) in semi-bold/medium to maximize glyph distinction when rendering kilohertz frequencies, depth markers, and sonar parameters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a fluid 12-column responsive grid calibrated to handle multidimensional acoustic views, split-pane spectrograms, and sensor feeds.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Layout supports complex docking panes, side-by-side hydrophone channel monitors, and persistent metric toolbars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Telemetry inspection panels collapse into bottom drawers or stacked tab views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column architecture with `1rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Single-column stacked metrics prioritizing acoustic waterfall charts and essential threshold controls.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on a rigid 4px/8px modular base scale (`0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`) controlling internal component paddings, card structures, and visual data blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or muddy drop-shadows to preserve scientific instrument clarity. Elevation is rendered through **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Low-Contrast Outlines** and **Hydro-Tinted Atmospheric Diffusions**.

- **Level 0 (Base Floor):** Pure `#ffffff`. Used for root application canvas and outer document wrappers.
- **Level 1 (Card & Rail Tier):** `#f8fafc` enclosed by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`). No drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Panels / Flyouts):** `#ffffff` elevated via a delicate cyan-tinted ambient halo: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.08)`. Border remains `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Global Overlays):** `#ffffff` sitting atop a translucent oceanic scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` backdrop blur `4px`). Elevation projection: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a calibrated `rounded-8` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) standard. This intermediate curve balances industrial efficiency with modern interface ergonomics.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to primary and secondary buttons, form inputs, chips, checkboxes, table cells, and status pills.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Applied to telemetry cards, floating toolbars, frequency docks, and data tables.
- **Modal Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Applied exclusively to central diagnostic modals, complex popover panels, and large overlay modules.
- **Full Radius (Pill):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status markers (e.g., live streaming signal badges, acoustic channel state indicators).

- **Secondary:** Background `#f1f5f9`, text `#0f172a`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Hover: background `#e2e8f0`, border-color `#cbd5e1`.
- **Solar Alert Button:** Background `#b45309`, text `#ffffff`. Hover: `#d97706`. Used exclusively for critical capture triggers, channel muting, or safety overrides.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `40px`, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, radius `8px`. Placeholder text `#334155` (60% opacity). Focus: border `1.5px solid #007b83`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 123, 131, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Size `18px`, radius `4px` (checkbox) / `50%` (radio), border `1.5px solid #0e7490`. Checked: background `#007b83` with sharp white tick icon. Focus ring: `#0d9488`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Acoustic Status Chip:** Padding `4px 10px`, radius `9999px` (pill), font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`.
- **Active Frequency Chip:** Background `rgba(0, 123, 131, 0.1)`, text `#007b83`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 123, 131, 0.2)`.
- **Telemetry Alert Chip:** Background `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.1)`, text `#b45309`, border `1px solid rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#f8fafc`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, corner radius `16px` (`rounded-lg`), padding `1.5rem`.
- **Card Header:** Sub-divided with a `1px solid #e2e8f0` bottom hairline, featuring an uppercase technical label (`label-sm`) in `#334155` and a primary metric readout in `#0f172a`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Sonar Waterfall / Spectrogram Canvas:** Contained in `#f8fafc` housing with `#e2e8f0` border; depth and frequency scale markers styled in `label-sm` with color `#334155`.
- **Channel Frequency Meter:** Horizontal segmented bar using `#007b83` for nominal signal levels, transitioning to `#b45309` when approaching hydrophone clipping thresholds.
